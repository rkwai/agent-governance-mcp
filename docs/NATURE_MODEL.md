# AI Nature Company

**Build a constitutional market of end-to-end offer cells. Eight natural forces operate through a deterministic MCP physics engine: Bound, Sense, Act, Transform, Maintain, Defend, Select, Continue. Organism, colony, and civilization emerge. Customer behavior supplies fitness. Capital selects winners. The founder holds the genome and the kill switch.**

The persistent organism is the **offer cell** — a complete hypothesis about how the company turns a customer problem into cash. The MCP substrate is the physics engine. Hosts and agents are temporary particles inside the forces, not departments and not a society of chatbots.

This README is the operational reading of [`docs/FOUNDING_CONVERSATION.md`](FOUNDING_CONVERSATION.md). That file is the founding philosophy. This file is the alignment target for the eight forces, the three scales they occupy, and what the MCP substrate must enforce.

The practical question:

> Can a company outperform a large centralized intelligence by combining simple, specialized effectors with clear incentives, fast market feedback, disciplined resource allocation, and strong safety boundaries?

---

## The MCP is the physics engine

Generative reasoning stays in the user-selected AI host. The MCP server does not think, schedule agents, or invoke a model. It owns identity, authorization, policy, budgets, state transitions, idempotency, evidence envelopes, audit, and rollback.

When the eight forces act through that substrate, three scales emerge. Hermetic: as above, so below — the same forces operate in similar *ways* at each scale.

| Scale | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Organism** | One offer cell: membrane, metabolism, and micro-P&L |
| **Colony** | The company portfolio: shared evidence, competing cells, allocation |
| **Civilization** | Founder genome over time: constitution, irreversible bets, gated learning |

Hosts, sit-the-seat principals, and labeled definitions are **effectors** — optional particles inside the forces. They are not the eight forces, not standing personas, and not an org chart. Do not create a chatbot society.

Founding topology remains:

> **One owner per outcome, temporary specialists, independent verification, and shared evidence.**

## The eight natural forces

These are persistent substrate law. Reward what the force is for. Do not reward activity that impersonates the force.

| Force | Law | Organism | Colony | Civilization |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bound** | Identity, membrane, permitted flows, fail-closed grants; inside vs outside; ICP and targets | Cell identity, ICP/targets, offer membrane, cell-scoped grants | Company constitution, membership, grants | Founder genome and irreversible authority |
| **Sense** | Observe world, market, and client signal into the shared medium **without** Act debit | Lists, enrich, packets inside Bound's ICP/target frame (`sense_market`) | Cross-cell evidence reads | Accumulated verified market history |
| **Act** | Side effects that change the world **cost** tries or budget | Leased reflex step with recorded cost | Portfolio experiments that spend exploration or exploitation | Founder-gated irreversible bets |
| **Transform** | Sense and Act must metabolize into structured evidence or economics, or they do not count as metabolism | Cell evidence and economics envelopes | Shared pheromone ledger | Institutional memory candidates |
| **Maintain** | Homeostasis: leases, overdue repair, harness health | Work leases and overdue open work | Process-owned `ai-nature-tick` hygiene | Regulatory and security posture |
| **Defend** | Independent verify; producer cannot clear its own claims | Evaluator ≠ executor of the same experiment | Independent closeout across cells | Human verification of irreversible action |
| **Select** | Allocation from fitness, not activity | Cell micro-P&L after eligibility gates | Allocator ranks cells; owner-led contain is not portfolio Select | Founder chooses arena, capital, and kill |
| **Continue** | Apoptosis, evidence retention, gated learning, decay | Stop rules, failed-experiment retention, gated lessons | Pause, kill, seed bank; evidence stays inspectable | Constitution versions, rollback, decay of obsolete strategy |

### Incentives aligned to forces

| Force | Reward it for | Do not reward it for |
|---|---|---|
| Bound | Intact membrane and fail-closed grants | Identity theater or expanding seats |
| Sense | Signals that later become validated demand | Observation volume or lead lists |
| Act | Collected contribution profit and verified outcomes | Packets sent, bookings, or tasks completed |
| Transform | Structured evidence and economics Select can read | Unmetabolized activity counts |
| Maintain | Repaired leases and a healthy harness | Ticket volume |
| Defend | Calibration and cheap falsification | Declaring experiments successful |
| Select | Portfolio profit, survival, and learning efficiency | Number of active agents or cells |
| Continue | Future performance uplift from gated learning; cheap apoptosis | Memory volume or rewriting failures as wins |

A negative result is successful when it cheaply kills an expensive false assumption.

## The offer cell is the organism

Each cell owns the complete economic loop. It does not hand work from a marketing seat to a sales department to an operations department.

A cell owns:

- A narrowly defined customer and problem
- The promise and proof
- The acquisition channel
- Pricing and commercial terms
- Delivery
- Cash collection
- Customer outcomes
- Delivery cost
- Lessons and reusable assets
- Its own micro-P&L

A cell survives only when it repeatedly creates customer value with attractive, attributable economics. The market selects. Capital amplifies winners. Learning changes the next generation.

## Effectors are particles, not the architecture

Roles remain permission sets. Labeled definitions (scout, designer-as-owner, sales, delivery, evaluator, memory, allocator) and live sit-the-seat principals (owner, specialist, evaluator) **submit to forces**. They are optional effectors. Do not mint eight standing personas to "be" the forces. Immune function is Bound + Defend policy in the substrate, not a chat agent. Strategy synthesis is advisory inside Continue and founder-owned.

### Sit-the-seat alignment

Live bindings remain owner / specialist / evaluator principals. Sense is a **second specialist effector path** (`sense_market`), not a fourth live principal and not a new force.

| Effector path | Effectuates | Must stay true |
|---|---|---|
| Owner | Bound — ICP, targets, cell membrane, offer lineage | Does not impersonate Select. Owner-led allocate/contain is single-cell containment, not portfolio ranking. Bound learns ICP / target / offer adaptation (wrong-person, wrong-promise, silence → retarget). |
| Sense specialist | Sense — research (`sense_market`) | Lists, enrich, and packets **inside Bound's ICP/target frame**. No try debit. Learns list quality, enrich quality, door truth, and buyer-identity signal — not list volume. |
| Act specialist (hunter) | Act — outbound / try-spend (`scout_market`, `sales_step`, `delivery_step`) | Sends only from Sense-ready packets. Founder signs every external send; Act is the send effector, not a bypass of sign-off. Complete/fail spends tries when cost is positive. Bound records each founder-signed send with `experiment.record_send` (exactly one try). Learns try effectiveness after Sense-ready (reply quality, door conversion) — not list volume. |
| Evaluator | Defend | Producer cannot clear its own claims. Evaluator ≠ executor. |

Bound, Sense, and Act learning loops stay **distinct**. Do not merge Sense research and Act outbound into one particle. Routine Sense↔Act↔Bound handoffs are MCP writes plus reads. Do not SendToAgent or DM a peer for those hops.

| Offer designer / cell owner | Bound (ICP, targets, offer lineage) | Improvement over a registered baseline | Idea volume |
| Sales | Act | Collected contribution profit adjusted for outcomes and retention | Bookings or contract value alone |
| Delivery | Act | Verified outcome per delivery cost, plus reusable assets | Tasks completed |
| Experiment evaluator | Defend | Calibration and cheap falsification | Declaring experiments successful |
| Memory | Continue | Future performance uplift from reused knowledge | Amount of memory written |
| Allocator | Select | Portfolio profit, survival, and learning efficiency | Number of active agents |

## Routines the forces already run

Faster loops do not wait for slower ones unless they cross a Bound constitution boundary.

| Routine | Cadence | Forces | What may change | Runtime |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reflex | Minutes to hours | Sense, Act | Next observation or world-changing step | Leased work. `sense_market` does not debit tries. `scout_market`, `sales_step`, `delivery_step` do. Not `ai-nature-tick`. |
| Adaptation | Daily | Bound, Transform | Copy, targeting, sequence, workflow parameters | `offer.mutate` creates an immutable child. Price floor, company promise, and security posture stay Bound. |
| Selection | Weekly | Select, Defend | Budget, priority, cloning, termination | Allocator + `fitness-v1`. Eligibility before rank. Select reads fitness, not packets. |
| Evolution | Monthly, or after strong evidence | Continue, Bound | ICP, offer architecture, business model, strategy | Founder. Advisory synthesis may propose; the founder approves genome-level mutation. |

Concrete Bound examples:

- A sales effector can test two subject lines without executive approval (Act).
- An offer cell can change a landing page within a defined claim boundary (Bound + Transform).
- It cannot cut the price below a margin floor.
- It cannot materially change the company’s promise or security posture.
- Advisory synthesis can propose entering healthcare; the founder approves that mutation.

## What MCP enforces

Agents coordinate by changing shared state, not by holding meetings. The MCP substrate is digital pheromones plus the gates that keep the organism honest. Identity is server-resolved. Caller-supplied actor, role, company, cell, budget, or entitlement fields are never authority.

**Write ledger → next force reads.** After a mutate that advances cell, run, evidence, lease, or tries, the default next step is **stop** or read-back via `company.describe` / `cell_records` — not a peer DM or agent chat. Shared MCP state is the force handoff. Grok chat is not the ledger. Agent-to-agent chat is out-of-band: founder-facing exceptions, true blockers, and digests the founder asked for. Channel membership is not a work queue. Force-peer silence is not silence toward everyone: founder, humans, and non-force agents stay on normal chat; acknowledge and reply when they are waiting.

`protocol.describe` advertises the eight forces as physics, names Sense vs Act spend, maps sit-the-seat principals as effectors — not as an eight-persona org chart — and encodes the ledger-handoff default plus the distinct external/host-chat obligation.

### Bound

Constitution, grants, and fail-closed identity. Inside vs outside is server-owned.

### Sense vs Act

- Sense writes observation into the shared medium and **must not** increment try `spent_minor`.
- Act complete/fail **must** debit tries or budget when the work carries side-effect cost.
- Discovery reads and `evidence.append` of Sense units (`demand_hint`, `market_signal`, `observed_behavior`, `world_signal`) are observation, not Act spend.

### Transform

Activity that is not metabolized into proper evidence or economics stays distinguishable. Cell and portfolio projections count `activity_evidence_count` vs `fitness_evidence_count`. Select reads fitness, not packets.

Every signal carries source, timestamp, offer cell, strength, confidence, causal distance from revenue, and expiration or half-life. Signals decay.

Cash collected ranks above renewal or retained usage, then signed commitment, verified outcome, proposal requested, meeting attended, positive reply, then click or page view. Negative pheromones are first-class: refund, delivery overrun, customer complaint, unexpected manual work, poor retention, security exception.

Revenue, payment, product-usage, and delivery facts come from authoritative systems or explicitly labeled manual attestations — not unsupported agent claims.

### Maintain and Continue

`ai-nature-tick` is Maintain (expired leases, overdue open work) and Continue (experiment closeout, claimable independent evaluation work). It never invokes a model and is not an agent scheduler. Nothing runs it on a schedule. State stays accurate by construction (a cell kill ends the cell's agent runs in the same transaction), and the Maintain/Continue scan is the on-demand `maintenance.run` tool for a founder or cell owner (report-only unless `end_runs_on_killed_cells` is set).

### Defend

The evaluator cannot be the executor of the same experiment. An execution agent cannot be the sole evaluator of its own work.

### Select

Eligibility and safety gates run before fitness ranking. Exploration and exploitation ledgers stay separate. Failed experiments remain inspectable. Agent activity, message volume, and task completion are not company-level success measures.

### Founder authority (Bound + Continue)

The founder retains constitution changes, irreversible kill, major capital commitments, high-risk market entry, and promotion of strategy-level mutations.

## Thin hierarchy

```text
Founder constitution          Bound / Continue
        ↓
Capital and risk rules        Bound / Select
        ↓
Competing offer cells  ↔  market evidence
        ↓
Selection and strategic update   Select / Continue
```

Strategy exists before and after execution. Before, it defines the arena, resource limits, and prohibited behavior. After, it becomes an updated model of where the company should compete. It should rarely prescribe the detailed actions of edge effectors.

KPIs are instrumentation through the organism, not a stage that issues commands.

The executive layer does not run the company. It shapes the environment in which profitable behavior emerges.

## Topology rule

Do not create a society of chatbots. Multi-agent debate is not the architecture.

| Task | Shape |
|---|---|
| Sequential, tightly coupled, context-heavy | One capable agent with tools and skills |
| Independent market exploration | Multiple parallel Sense/Act effectors |
| Dynamic environment with local feedback | Decentralized offer cells using shared state |
| Cross-cell resource conflict | Central Select (allocator) |
| High-stakes or irreversible action | Central Defend and human approval |
| Adversarial evaluation | Independent producer and verifier |
